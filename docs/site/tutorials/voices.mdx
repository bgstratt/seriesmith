  Voices and your own dialogue: who says the lines, and how they sound.
</Card>

How dialogue sounds is the series config's **Dialogue** setting (`audio.mode`):

| Dialogue | What you get | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| **generate** | The model makes up a voice from each character's `voice` words, every shot | Nothing |
| **clone** | The written lines spoken in each character's own voice sample, the same in every shot | A voice sample for everyone who speaks |
| **recorded track** | Each shot lip-syncs to your own recording of the lines | One recording; faster-whisper to align it |

This continues on the Dean episode (four lines between Dean and Pip).

## 1. A voice sample

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the voice">
    A character's card (Refs, or Series settings' cast) has **Voice**, in words: *a bright,
    gentle little boy's voice*. With **generate**, that's all the model gets, so the voice is
    made up anew each shot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a sample">
    Refs → the character's **Voice sample** row → **Generate a sample**: a few seconds of that
    voice, made on LTX-2.5 (audio only). Generate a few and listen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or import one">
    Drop a recording of the voice you want on the slot (or **Upload…**), or **Use a line from a
    take…** to take it from a take you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick it">
    **Pick** the one to use. It's written to `refs/voices/` and its `voice_sample` line added to
    the series config.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Refs' **Generate missing** makes the pictures, not the voices: generate each voice from its
  own row. Generated samples need LTX-2.5's files (**Settings → Features…**, *Voices*).
</Note>

## 2. Cloned voices

<Steps>
  <Step title="A sample for everyone who speaks">
    Each speaking character needs a picked voice sample (part 1).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn cloning on">
    For the whole series: **Series settings** → **Dialogue** → **clone**, and Save. For one
    shot: `policy: clone` on its lines in the script.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-render">
    The dialogue shots are now stale: **Re-render stale**. Same seeds, so only the voice
    differs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare">
    **Compare with…** each shot's old and new take: before, a voice made up for that shot;
    after, cloned from the sample.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Your own recording

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install faster-whisper (once)">
    Align transcribes the recording with Whisper. Install it into the Python that runs
    seriesmith:

    ```
    pip install faster-whisper
    ```

    (Inside ComfyUI, that's ComfyUI's Python; for the page of its own, the repo's `.venv`.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the lines">
    All the episode's lines, **in script order**, in one file, with a pause between each and
    real pauses where the script has silent shots. Dry: no music under them. A phone is fine.
    For this episode:
    *Are you lost, little owl?* · *I can't see my tree.* · *Follow the stars, Pip!* · *Goodnight, Pip.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach it">
    Open **Recording** (the speaker icon in the Shots toolbar). Under **Attach a recording**,
    browse to the file (**Browse the ComfyUI machine…**) or type its path and **Use this
    path**. It's copied into the episode's `audio/`, and the episode now dubs to it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dry run, then Align">
    **Dry run** shows what align would write without writing it. Then **Align**: it transcribes
    the recording, finds each line's window, and matches it to its shot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the report">
    Every line, its shot and its window. Align writes `audio: in-out` on each shot (as long as
    you took to say it), sets the series config's dialogue to the recorded track, leaves
    `align_report.md` beside the episode, and rebuilds. The old files go to `_history/`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-render">
    **Re-render stale**: each dialogue shot lip-syncs to your line, keeping the model's own
    sound effects under it (the default, `dub_keep_foley`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play it with your voice">
    **Play all** → **recording**: your voice under the cut.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Frame lip-sync shots as close or medium shots: the picture's framing matters more than the
  prompt. Replacing the recording? Attach the new one and align again.
</Tip>
