  Continuity: two shots that carry on as one.
</Card>

Usually every shot starts fresh: people re-pose, the light shifts, the camera resets. For a
shot that carries straight on from the one before (the same moment, the camera still
running), one line says so:

| Line | What it does |
|---|---|
| `continuous: first` | The shot opens on the last frame of the shot before. The picture carries over; the movement and sound start again |
| `continuous: latent` | The end of the shot before is held at the start of this one, and the model carries on from it: picture, motion and sound (H3 Ref2VA and FastH3 Omni) |

"The shot before" is the one before it **in the cut**: move a shot on the timeline and it
continues from its new neighbour.

## 1. Set up an episode to compare them

This makes *Dean's Lullaby*: the same two moments twice, joined one way in sq01 and the other
in sq02.

<Steps>
  <Step title="A new episode">
    **Project folders** → the Dean show's folder → **New episode…** (`ep02`). It gets its own
    copy of the series config and shares the show's `refs` folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the script">
    Open the **Script** window and replace what's there:

    ```
    = ep02  Dean's Lullaby

    # sq01  window_seat

    ## sh010
    who: dean
    size: medium
    dur: 4
    Dean sits on the window seat humming a lullaby and slowly lifts the glowing star nightlight up toward the round window.
    camera: holds a static shot
    sound: a small boy softly humming a lullaby, a quiet night room

    ## sh020
    who: dean
    size: medium
    dur: 4
    continuous: first
    Dean keeps humming and holds the star nightlight against the glass, its warm glow spreading across the window.
    camera: holds a static shot
    sound: a small boy softly humming a lullaby, a quiet night room

    # sq02  window_seat

    ## sh030
    who: dean
    size: medium
    dur: 4
    Dean sits on the window seat humming a lullaby and slowly lifts the glowing star nightlight up toward the round window.
    camera: holds a static shot
    sound: a small boy softly humming a lullaby, a quiet night room

    ## sh040
    who: dean
    size: medium
    dur: 4
    continuous: latent
    Dean keeps humming and holds the star nightlight against the glass, its warm glow spreading across the window.
    camera: holds a static shot
    sound: a small boy softly humming a lullaby, a quiet night room
    ```

    **Save**, then **Build**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. continuous: first

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate missing">
    Press **Generate missing**. A continuing shot waits for the shot before it: in **Jobs**,
    sh020 says it's waiting for sh010, and starts from sh010's new take once it's in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Where it renders">
    sh020 opens on sh010's last frame. FastH3 Omni and H3 Ref2VA don't read a first frame, so
    seriesmith renders sh020 on **H3 FL2VA** by itself. (Any model that reads a first frame
    can do `first`, hosted ones included.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play sq01">
    Right-click sh010 → **Play from here**. The picture carries over the cut; the movement
    and the humming start again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. continuous: latent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Its render waits too">
    sh040 waits for sh030 the same way.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What it does">
    The end of sh030's take (39 frames by default, `overlap:` to change it) is held at the
    start of sh040, and the model generates the rest. sh040 keeps its own pictures and
    prompt; the held frames are trimmed after.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play sq02">
    The hum and the hand carry straight on across the cut: one movement, one sound.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 4. Side by side

**Play all**: sq01, joined by its first frame, then sq02, by its latent. Compare each join
with a wipe (**Compare with…**).

<Note>
  Live playback can blink at a join. The assembled episode (**Export**, or the master) is
  seamless.
</Note>

## In the script

- **On a shot:** `continuous: first` or `continuous: latent`, as above.
- **On a sequence's `#` header:** every shot after the sequence's first continues. It stops at
  the next `#`; `continuous: none` on a shot breaks the chain.
- **Across a sequence boundary:** only the shot itself can say it, on the first shot of the
  new sequence.
- **From the editor:** a take's menu has **Use this frame as the next shot's first frame**
  and **Use previous shot's last frame as first frame**, for a keyframe from a frame you choose.
