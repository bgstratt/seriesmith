{/* Written by hand. */}

From a blank episode to a watchable animatic, without writing a full script first. You'll pick a
story structure and a length, block the episode in with held shots, drop in pictures, and
render them for free on your own machine. Every field is covered in the
[storyboard guide](/storyboard).

## 1. Start the episode from a structure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the New Episode Wizard">
    Choose **New episode**, then under **Start from** press **Block from a structure**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the first steps">
    Name the episode and add its cast and places, as for any new episode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a structure and a length">
    On the **Structure** step choose **The Change Arc (Three-Act Structure)** and enter **7** minutes.
    If the series has no locations yet, say where it opens, in a few words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Press **Create**. The episode opens on the storyboard: three act rows, each beat a bar with
    a target such as *0 of 24 shots*, and no shots yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Already have an episode? Open it and press **Storyboard…** in the Shots tab. If its script has
  no beats, **Lay out beats** places the structure over the shots you have.
</Tip>

## 2. Block it in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the beats">
    The line under each bar says what should happen there. Hover over the bar for its place in
    the story.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put one shot on every beat">
    Press **One per beat** in the toolbar. Each beat now has a held stand-in, and the whole
    story exists from start to end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add what you need">
    Press **+** at the end of a beat to add a shot, or **Fill** to bring it up to its target.
    **Fill act** and **Fill all** do the same for more at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the places">
    Click a sequence's green bar to choose its place and cast. To change location partway through
    a beat, right-click a sequence's bar and choose **New sequence after…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it yours">
    Double-click a beat's name to rename it. Right-click a beat or act to add your own.
    Drag a tile to reorder it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. Make the animatic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Drop your pictures">
    Select your sketches or stills in a file window and drop them on a beat's bar. They fill its
    empty stand-ins in filename order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place single pictures">
    Drop one on the **middle** of a tile to replace that shot's frame, or on a tile's **edge** to
    make a new shot there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Render held">
    Choose **proxy** in the editor and press **Render held** in the storyboard's toolbar. It runs on
    this machine and costs nothing. Shots still on the gray card render too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it">
    Press **Play all** on the timeline. The animatic plays with every shot at its planned length.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  With beats in the script, the spotting sheet's **Draft…** can score the blocked episode, so you
  can judge the shape of the music before anything is written.
</Tip>

## 4. Turn stand-ins into real shots

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the shot">
    Double-click a tile to open Edit Shot and replace the beat's guidance with what happens in the shot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose its model">
    Pick the model for one shot in Edit Shot, or right-click a sequence, beat or act and choose
    **Convert held shots**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Render">
    Render as usual. A dropped picture becomes the first frame on a model that reads one, such
    as FL2VA.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Read your shots before you convert them. A stand-in you haven't rewritten still has the beat's
  guidance as its action, and a paid model charges for that render like any other.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Changed your mind? Click on the board and press **Ctrl+Z**. Every edit saves to the script at
  once, and undo steps back through them while the window is open.
</Tip>
