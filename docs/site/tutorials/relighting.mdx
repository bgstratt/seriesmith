  Relighting: the same places, under another light.

A relight is an edit of a picture that changes its light and keeps everything else: the
window seat at dusk, in snow, by candlelight. One plate at a time, or every plate of a
sequence at once, and then the shots re-rendered on the same seeds so only the light
differs.

**You need:** an edit model for pictures (FLUX.2 Klein edit, Qwen-Image 2.1, FLUX.3
Klein, Fibo 2.0...). This continues on the Dean episode.

## 1. One plate, relit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit window">
    **Refs** → the **window_seat** location → **Edit live…** (or **Edit** on a candidate).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a relight">
    The **Relight** row above the instruction shows the presets as pictures, each the same
    sample place under that light: time of day (dawn to night), weather (overcast, rain, fog,
    snow, storm) and light (neon, moonlight, streetlights, firelight, sunbeams...). Click one:
    it fills the instruction, which stays yours to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the instruction, then queue it">
    Read the words before you run them (see part 3), then press **Queue Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare">
    Each relight is a new candidate; the original stays. Compare them side by side, and press
    **Pick** on the one to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. A relight of your own

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the light yourself">
    In the same edit window, type your own instruction:

    ```
    warm candlelight from a single candle on the windowsill, deep amber glow, the rest of the room in soft shadow
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save as a relight…">
    Press **Save as a relight…** under the Relight row and name it (*candlelight*). It's
    offered under **Yours** in every show on this machine, in the edit window and on a
    sequence's Relight…. **✕** on its tile removes it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it a picture (optional)">
    Until it has one, its tile shows its words. On an edited take, **save as a relight…**
    beside its instruction keeps it with that take's picture as its tile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 3. When a relight adds things

A relight names where its light comes from, and the edit model can draw in what it names.
*Firelight* that mentions candles and a brazier gets candles; *dusk* with "the street lamps
lit" in a room with no street gets a street drawn outside; snow that "lies on every surface"
lies on the cushions indoors.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read what it says">
    Before **Queue Edit**, read the instruction against the picture. Does it name a lamp, a
    window, a fire, a street the picture hasn't got?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cut the word, don't add a no">
    Delete what isn't there. Don't add *no lamps*: that puts the word in all the same.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what's outside and what isn't">
    For weather, say where it is: *snow is falling outside the window, the yard and the tree
    covered in snow*, not *snow on every surface*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare with the original">
    Side by side, look for anything new besides the light.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 4. A whole sequence under another light

For a flashback, a dream or a night version: every plate a sequence uses, relit at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Relight… on the sequence">
    **Shots** → the sequence's header (sq01) → **Relight…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the light">
    Pick a relight picture (**Snow**, **Dusk**, one of **Yours**) or say it in your own words.
    The dialog lists what it will make before you start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point the sequence at the relit plates">
    Leave **Point sq01 at the relit plates** on: it rewrites the sequence's location and its
    shots' `plate:` lines so the next render uses the new plates (the script keeps a history
    copy). Press **Relight**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new plates">
    Each plate becomes a new location (`window_seat_dusk`), made from the plate itself, so the
    old one is still there. Pick the takes in Refs as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-render the sequence">
    On the sequence's header, **Re-render…**: every shot again as a new take, with the same
    seeds, so only the light has changed. Your old takes stay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  To go back, point the sequence's header and `plate:` lines at the original location in the
  Script window, Save, and re-render. Both versions of each shot stay among its takes.
</Tip>
