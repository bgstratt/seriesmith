Each tutorial is a short video on YouTube, and each page here is the same tutorial written
out as steps: every click, every field and what to type in it, in order. Watch the video
for the shape of it, then follow the page at your own pace.

They build on each other. Tutorials 1 and 2 make clips in Simple mode; from 3 on they
follow one episode, *Dean and the Little Lost Owl*, from its script to its finished master.
