title: "seriesmith"
description: "A series config and episode scripts in, finished episodes out: any shot on any model, on your own GPU or in the cloud."
---

You describe a show once in `series.json`: the cast, the places, the voices, the look. Then
you write each episode as a screenplay-flavoured markdown script (`epNN.md`). seriesmith
turns the script into shots, makes the reference pictures, keyframes and voices they need,
renders every shot on the model and machine you choose, and cuts the takes together through
upscale, post and master.

You drive it from the **editor**, a page in your browser (shots, takes, refs, the cut and the
queue), or from one command, `seriesmith`. Every shot is its own job, so a bad shot is
rendered again alone and the rest of the episode is untouched. Every take is kept with the
exact settings that made it.

## How it fits together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the show">
    `series.json` holds the cast, places, props and voices, and which models draw them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the episode">
    Each shot is a few lines: who is in it, the framing and length, what happens, what is said,
    the camera and the sound.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the pictures">
    Character sheets, location plates and keyframes, generated on an image model or supplied by you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Render and cut">
    Proxies first to judge the cut, then finals; pick takes, trim, and master with titles.
  </Step>
</Steps>
